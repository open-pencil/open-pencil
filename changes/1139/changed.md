- New logo (kit v5, https://github.com/rcoenen/openpencil-logo-kit): a P built from three flat pieces, a flame stem, a sun bowl, and a dusk foot, the pencil. The app icon, favicons, and PWA and desktop icons use it on an ink tile, and the one-colour mark is ink or white. The loading screen plays the mark assembling, its outlines drawing in, the pieces sliding together, and the colour wiping in, in full before the app opens; with reduced motion it shows the finished mark.
