- OpenPencil Cloud is ready to self-host, and to try before the hosted service opens: open the editor with `?cloud=preview`, or follow an invitation, to keep documents on your team's server and edit them together. Connect a server in **Settings → Cloud**, which also holds the hosted service's waitlist: email and password sign in, and create an account, right in the dialog, while Google, Apple, and two-step sign-in finish in the browser. Home lists the server's workspaces and the documents shared with you, every account starts with a workspace of its own and can create team workspaces whose admins invite people by email as admins, editors, or viewers and delete them once they are empty, **New design** creates in the place Home shows, **File → Save to…** saves the open document straight to the only workspace or bucket it can go to and asks only when there are several, **File → Browse files…** opens Home where you left it, and a status beside the name appears when the document is offline, couldn't save, changed in two places, with the choice to keep both versions, or is view only. A viewer's document is read-only, as in Figma: layers can be selected, copied, and exported, the toolbar keeps Move and Hand, and edits, from the canvas, panels, shortcuts, and agents alike, are refused. **File → Show version history** lists a document's named versions and autosaves, as in Figma: choose one to look at it read-only, then restore it for everyone in the room, duplicate it, or edit or delete its info, and **File → Save to version history…** (⌥⌘S) names the document as it is now. In the web app the address bar shows the open Cloud document, and the version being looked at, so a reload or the address reopens it for anyone with access, and a version's **Copy link** copies that address. Opening a Cloud document joins its live room, so everyone with it open sees edits and cursors as they happen while one editor saves for all. **Share** invites people by email and turns on a link that opens the document for guests, within what the workspace's plan allows; on a computer, the web editor offers to open invitations and shared links in the desktop app. The server, its relay, and its pages for signing in, account security, and administration are in `@open-pencil/cloud` under the Apache License 2.0, while the protocol the editor speaks to it stays MIT; `VITE_OPENPENCIL_CLOUD=off` builds the editor without Cloud.
