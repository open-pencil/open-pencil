- The Vue SDK reads messages with `useMessages(namespace)` and locale state with `useLocale()`, both from `@open-pencil/vue/i18n`; `useI18n()`, the `use*Messages()` composables, and the per-catalog stores such as `panelMessages` are removed, and code outside components uses `messageStores`. A namespace is named by its catalog's path, so `cloud`, `cloudPortal`, `designCheck`, `variables`, and `variableTypes` are now `cloud/editor`, `cloud/portal`, `design-check`, `variables/panel`, and `variables/types`, and the picker, library, behaviour, and Cloud console messages moved out of `panels` and `cloud/portal` into namespaces of their own, such as `panels/pickers` and `cloud/portal/tokens`.
