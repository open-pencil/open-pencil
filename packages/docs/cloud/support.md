# Support

## The Hosted Service

For help with your account or documents on `cloud.openpencil.dev`, write to [support@openpencil.dev](mailto:support@openpencil.dev) from the email address of your account, and include your account ID from **Profile** if you have it.

| Address                                                    | For                                                     |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| [support@openpencil.dev](mailto:support@openpencil.dev)    | Your account, documents, workspaces, and plans          |
| [abuse@openpencil.dev](mailto:abuse@openpencil.dev)        | Content that breaks the [Terms of Service](./legal/terms) |
| [privacy@openpencil.dev](mailto:privacy@openpencil.dev)    | Your personal data and your rights under the [Privacy Policy](./legal/privacy) |
| [legal@openpencil.dev](mailto:legal@openpencil.dev)        | Legal notices                                           |

To report a shared document, use **Report this file** in the document instead; see [Report a Shared Document](./sharing#report-a-shared-document).

## Problems with the Editor

For a bug in the editor itself, use **File → Report a Problem…**, or open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/open-pencil/open-pencil/issues). Discussion and questions are welcome on [Discord](https://discord.gg/4wXc9fuZfm).

## A Server Your Team Runs

Its administrators look after accounts and documents on it; ask them. Your account ID, under **Profile** on the server's account pages, helps them find your account. Their terms and privacy policy, when they have them, are linked under the server's sign-in buttons.
