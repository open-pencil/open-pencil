- A Cloud server's console lists people, access requests, email, and activity as tables with labelled columns, and each one is searched, filtered with a count per filter, and paged: requests by name or email, email by recipient and delivery, activity by who acted or whom it was about. Access requests name the administrator who decided them, and each row keeps one action at hand with the rest in its "…" menu. Pages show placeholders while they first load and keep their rows while they load again, and the section row on a narrow screen scrolls with a chevron instead of a scrollbar.
