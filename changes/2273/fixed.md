- Display P3 documents export their colors as they are: CSS, HTML, and JSX write `color(display-p3 …)` and blend gradients in Display P3, SVG writes P3 colors or the nearest sRGB ones when exported as sRGB, `exportAsync` follows `colorProfile`, design tokens record their color space, and PPTX, draw.io, and PDF bring colors inside sRGB instead of reading P3 values as sRGB; color contrast lint measures them in the document's profile (#2273).
