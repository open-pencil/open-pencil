- Cloud's AI service is now OpenPencil services, which will also cover image generation and search, all paid from the same credits. In the Cloud TOML, rename `[ai]` to `[services]` and `[entitlements.ai]` to `[entitlements.services]`, and rename the `OPENPENCIL_CLOUD_AI_SIGNING_KEY` secret to `OPENPENCIL_CLOUD_SERVICES_SIGNING_KEY`. Entitlements stored in the database move over on their own. A service now accepts tokens with the audience `openpencil-services`, and editors request them from `POST /api/services/token`. The AI relay ships with Cloud and upgrades with it. Editors from earlier betas lose the OpenPencil AI provider against an upgraded Cloud until they update.
