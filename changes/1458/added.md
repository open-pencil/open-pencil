- A Cloud server's account pages add **Profile**, to change your name and, through a link sent to the new address, your email, and **Sessions**, which lists where your account is signed in and its recent sign-ins and signs out any of them. The server emails you about a sign-in from a new address and about changes to two-step sign-in. Administrators filter and page through people, open a page for each account, reset a locked-out person's two-step sign-in, and give a reason when suspending someone. A workspace's **Members** dialog splits into Members, Invited, and Settings tabs, showing who sent each invitation and when it expires, with **Resend**.
