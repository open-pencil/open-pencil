- Documents shared by link and community gallery documents can be embedded in other sites with an iframe: **Copy embed code** in Share gives the frame, which shows the document read-only and never uses the visitor's account; a shared document keeps up with edits as they happen, and a community document shows its latest version or the one its address names. Hosted Cloud allows embeds from any site; self-hosted servers turn them on under `[deployment.embedding]`, optionally limited to listed origins.
- `@open-pencil/collab` opens collaboration rooms for any host: `openCollabRoom` keeps a room's document in step with an editor over a transport, and `@open-pencil/cloud/client` joins Cloud rooms with `createCloudRelayJoin` and `cloudTicketRelayJoin`.
