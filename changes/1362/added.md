- The AI assistant can use models through OpenPencil Cloud: choose **OpenPencil Cloud** as a model's provider in **Settings → AI** and the workspace whose plan pays for it, with the models and prices the server offers. When the workspace's AI credits for the month run out, the assistant says so and offers **Manage plan** where the server bills.
- Self-hosted OpenPencil Cloud servers can offer AI through the AI relay, `dist-standalone/ai-relay.mjs`, which forwards requests to one OpenAI-compatible provider with your key and holds each workspace to its monthly credit allowance.
