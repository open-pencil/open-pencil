- `@open-pencil/cloud/server` serves the Cloud API from an oRPC contract: the route factories for documents, versions, comments, sharing, collaboration tickets, reports, workspaces, community data, services and billing tokens, and the internal API give way to `create…Procedures` and `create…RateLimits`, and `CloudAPI`, `PublicCloudAPI`, `CloudAdminRoutes`, and `adminErrorStatus` are gone. `staticEntitlementsSchema` and `setAccountEntitlementsSchema` move to `@open-pencil/cloud/contract`.
- `@open-pencil/cloud/contract` describes the services and billing APIs as `servicesContract` and `billingContract` in place of `CloudServicesAPI`, `CloudServicesSchema`, `CloudBillingAPI`, and `CloudBillingSchema`; `@open-pencil/cloud/client` drops `CloudRequestOptions.onDiagnostic` and `CloudClientDiagnostic`.
