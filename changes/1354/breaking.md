- Scripts use variables as in Figma's Plugin API: `figma.variables` creates and looks up variables and collections, variables set values with `setValueForMode`, and layers bind with `node.setBoundVariable` and paint `boundVariables`; the top-level `figma.getVariableById`, `createVariable`, `setVariableValue`, `deleteVariable` and collection methods are removed.
- Scripts reach OpenPencil-only features through `openpencil`: `exposeInstanceSwap`, `getFontStatus`, `getFillOkHCL`/`setFillOkHCL` and the stroke pair, and `get`/`setLayoutDirection` and `get`/`setTextDirection` take a node; `figma.booleanOperation` and `figma.flattenNode` are removed in favour of Figma's `figma.union`, `subtract`, `intersect`, `exclude` and `flatten`.
