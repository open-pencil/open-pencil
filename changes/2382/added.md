- Present Figma Slides decks full screen, from the current slide or the start, with Figma's slide transitions and layer animations that play click by click, and controls on the slides that stay live. A presenter's window shows the speaker notes, the next slide, the build step, a clock, and a timer, and moves the deck. A design's frames present as slides too.
- Animate decks from an Animate tab: set a slide's transition with its style, direction, duration, easing, and timing, and make layers fade or slide in or out on a click or after the step before. Transition and animation durations and easings can follow timing and easing variables, kept in the `.deck` file.
- Give agents tools to outline a deck and to create and move slides, set their notes, skipping, and transitions, and animate their layers. They are listed only while a deck is open, so agents working on designs and boards carry none of them, and `list_documents` reports each document's kind.
