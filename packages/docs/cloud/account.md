# Your Account

A server's account pages have three parts:

- **Profile** shows your picture, name, and email, and your account ID, which an administrator may ask for. **Upload picture**, beside your picture, takes a PNG, JPEG, WebP, or GIF image; drag and zoom it to choose what shows in the circle, and **Remove**, once you confirm, goes back to your initials. **Change name** opens a dialog for your new name. Signing in with Google uses your Google picture until you upload one. The server keeps every picture itself, so pages never load them from Google or anywhere else. **Change email** sends a link to the new address, and your email changes once you open it. The old address gets a note saying which address is taking over. If you signed in a while ago, the server asks for your password first.
- **Sign-in and security** links Google or Apple, changes your password, and sets up two-step sign-in with an authenticator app or passkeys. Recovery codes are shown once: copy or download them before you close the dialog.
- **Sessions** lists every browser and desktop app signed in to your account, with when each was last active, and your recent sign-ins. Sign out any session you don't recognize, or all of them except the one you're using.

The server emails you when your account is signed in from an address it has never been signed in from, and when an authenticator app or a passkey is added or removed.

## Accept the Terms

A server can ask you to accept its terms. Signing up shows a line saying that continuing means agreeing to them, with links to the terms and, where the server has one, its privacy policy. When the server has a version you haven't accepted, on your first sign-in or after it changes its terms, its pages open on **Accept the terms to continue** before anything else:

- **Accept and continue** records which version you accepted and when, and takes you on.
- **Decline and sign out** signs you out. You're asked again the next time you sign in.
- **Leave with your documents**, under **Don't want to accept?**, lets you download your files and delete your account without accepting.

Until you accept, the editor can't open the server's documents, and **Settings → Cloud** says **Accept this server's terms to keep using it**, with **Review terms** to open that page.

## Download Your Files and Delete Your Account

**Delete account…**, at the bottom of **Profile**, lists what goes before you confirm: the workspaces only you belong to, with their files; your membership in shared workspaces, whose files stay with the others; and your sign-in methods, sessions, and picture. It can't be undone, and if you signed in a while ago the server asks for your password first.

- **Download all** saves every file you'd lose in one zip first, and names any it couldn't download.
- If you are the only admin of a workspace other people still use, make one of them an admin first. The server's only administrator has to make someone else a server administrator first too.

On the hosted service, the [Privacy Policy](./legal/privacy#deleting) says how long deleted files are kept before they are removed for good. A self-hosted server's operator sets its own periods.
