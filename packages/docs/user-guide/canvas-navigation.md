# Canvas Navigation

The canvas has no edges. Move around it and zoom in and out to work on any part of your design.

## Move Around

- Scroll with two fingers on a trackpad, or with the mouse wheel. Hold <Keys mac="⇧" other="Shift" /> to scroll sideways.
- Hold <Keys mac="Space" other="Space" /> and drag.
- Drag with the middle mouse button.

To drag the canvas without holding a key, press <Keys mac="H" other="H" /> for the hand tool. Press <Keys mac="V" other="V" /> to go back to selecting.

## Zoom

- Pinch on a trackpad, or hold <Keys mac="⌘" other="Ctrl" /> and scroll. The canvas zooms in on the pointer.
- Press <Keys mac="+" other="+" /> or <Keys mac="−" other="−" />.

Click the percentage at the top of the Design panel for more:

<GuideLegend>

<GuideImage name="canvas-navigation/zoom" other="canvas-navigation/zoom-other" alt="The zoom menu with zoom options, Pixel grid, Rulers, and other view settings" />

- **Zoom to fit** <Keys mac="⇧ 1" other="Shift 1" /> shows everything on the page.
- **Zoom to 100%** <Keys mac="⌘ 0" other="Ctrl 0" /> shows your design at its real size.
- Type a percentage in the field at the top.

</GuideLegend>

To fill the canvas with what you selected, press <Keys mac="⇧ 2" other="Shift 2" />.

## Jump Between Frames and Pages

- Press <Keys mac="N" other="N" /> to go to the next frame on the page, and <Keys mac="⇧ N" other="Shift N" /> to go back. The frame is selected and fills the canvas.
- Press <Keys mac="Page Down" other="Page Down" /> or <Keys mac="Page Up" other="Page Up" /> to open the next or previous page.

Each page remembers where you were the last time you saw it.

## Measure Distances

1. Select a layer.
2. Hold <Keys mac="⌥" other="Alt" /> and point at another layer.

<GuideImage name="canvas-navigation/measure" alt="Low Tide selected and a red 16 between it and Paper Lanterns" />

The red numbers are the distances between them. Let go of the key to hide them.

## Use Rulers and Guides

Rulers run along the top and left of the canvas. Press <Keys mac="⇧ R" other="Shift R" /> to hide or show them.

Guides are lines that help you line things up. They aren't part of your design.

1. Drag from the top ruler for a horizontal guide, or from the left ruler for a vertical one.
2. Let go over a frame to make the guide part of that frame, or anywhere else for a guide on the page.

<GuideImage name="canvas-navigation/guide" alt="A blue guide across two cards, with its position on the left ruler" />

Drag a guide to move it, or hold <Keys mac="⌥" other="Alt" /> while you drag to copy it. To remove a guide, drag it back onto the ruler, or right-click it and choose **Remove guide**.

## Snap Into Place

Layers you move, resize, or draw snap to the edges and centers of layers around them, and to guides. To move a layer without snapping, hold <Keys mac="⌃" other="Ctrl" /> while you drag.

In the zoom menu, **Snap to pixel grid** puts layers on whole pixels. Choose **View → Preferences** to turn off snapping to other layers.

When you zoom in far enough, the canvas shows a line between every pixel. Turn it off with **Pixel grid** in the zoom menu.
