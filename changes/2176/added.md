- The Icon section sets how Material Symbols and SF Symbols are drawn: a Material Symbol's optical size, weight, fill, and grade, and an SF Symbol's weight, scale, rendering, and variable value. SF Symbols add palette rendering, with secondary and tertiary colors, and multicolor rendering, in Apple's own colors. A placed Material Symbol starts at the optical size of its size. `insert_icon` and Design JSX `<Icon>` take the same `options`. Where SF Symbols can't be drawn, in the browser, on Windows and Linux, and on macOS before 14, the icon picker and the Icon section say what they need (#2176).
